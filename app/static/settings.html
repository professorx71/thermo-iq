Text Message Alerts

Texts are sent as email to each phone's carrier email-to-text address, e.g. 6045551234@txt.bell.ca. Keep texts short; carriers may delay or drop them, so always confirm with Send Test Text.

No-Data Alarm and Escalation

No-data alarm: one email and text to the recipients above when a logger sends no readings, or LI-COR Cloud cannot be reached, for this long, and one when data is back. It ignores the alarm schedule; loggers in NOT IN USE are skipped.

Escalation: when nobody acknowledges a temperature alarm in time, the supervisor list below is also told, once per alarm. Supervisors then also hear when it is acknowledged and when it clears.

Email reports — daily, weekly, monthly and calibration reminders

Daily: maintenance report plus an Excel workbook of every logger's temperature (hourly min / avg / max chart). Weekly and monthly: QA workbook with daily min / avg / max per logger. Calibration: reminder email before each logger's next-due date. All go to the recipients below.
